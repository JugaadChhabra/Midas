How these are measured

CTR before = clicks ÷ impressions over the window ending just before the change.
CTR after = the same ratio over an equal-length window starting just after it.
CTR change = (after − before) ÷ before × 100, judged once the post window closes.
Click-through rate is used rather than views because view counts decay steeply after upload: any before/after on raw views reports a large drop for every video regardless of what changed. CTR is a ratio over matched windows, so it is not distorted by that decay.
