How to read this
  • Result is the answer: Helping, No clear effect or Hurting comes from the weekly judgement (Mondays). Stopped means it didn't help there, so Midas switched it off on that channel.
  • Lift is how much more a changed video gained than the videos Midas deliberately left alone (1 in 5 is held back for comparison). Backlinks: extra views per week arriving from the linking video. Titles: the extra change in browse, search and suggested views, as a %.
  • early in italics is a first look at changes still being measured. It is not a verdict and never stops or undoes anything.
