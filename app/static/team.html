to

Your team members. They have no login: the roster is what channels are assigned to, so each upload is credited to whoever held the channel that day. Deactivate someone who has left; their past uploads stay credited.

Channels

Who uploads to each channel. Picking someone assigns the channel to them from today (IST). The first person given a channel is also credited with its earlier uploads; after a change, uploads before today stay credited to whoever held it then.
